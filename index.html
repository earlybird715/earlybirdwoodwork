About My Craft

About Me

My name is Early Walter, I am a 17 year old, born and raised in Boise, Idaho. I have always had a passion for woodworking, art, design, and engineering.

Woods beauty has always fascinated me, when you look at the grain It tells a story about the trees life, and how it grew. Pairing it with the rigid lines cut by the saws is not only visually interesting, but reveals the story in a specific way.

I hope you like my pieces, reach out to me with any questions or requests, thank you :)
